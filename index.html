Who is playing?

📖 How to play

Practice the times tables from 1 × 1 to 10 × 10. The game gets harder as you get better, and easier if you need more practice.

Playing

  • Pick your name on the start screen, or type a new one, then press Play.
  • After a 3, 2, 1, Go! countdown, type the answer with the number keys and press Enter or Check.
  • You find out straight away if it's right. A green card means right, a red card shows the correct answer.
  • Keep going as long as you like. Press S or Stop to finish and see your report.
  • If nobody presses anything for 45 seconds, the game stops by itself and keeps your stars.

📈 Levels

  • The Next level bar fills as you answer: a fast right answer adds the most, and a wrong answer takes some away.
  • There are 50 levels. New tables join as you go up: ×1, ×10, ×2, ×5, ×3, ×4, ×6, ×9, ×7 and finally ×8 at level 17. The levels in between mix all your tables together.
  • After every level you get a summary and a break. Press Enter to keep playing, or S to stop.
  • If you get a lot wrong, you go back a level for a bit of extra practice.
  • At level 50 you stay at the top, and every 30 points brings another summary.

★ Your chart

  • Answer a fact right 3 times in a row to earn its ★ on your multiplication chart. 3 × 7 and 7 × 3 are separate facts.
  • Facts with a ★ still come back now and then, but much less often than the ones you're still learning.
  • Your report (the 📊 button) shows the chart, your speed on every fact, and which facts need practice.
  • Star all 100 facts to learn the whole multiplication table! 🏆

🔥 Insane mode

  • Turn it on before you press Play. Each question has a 5-second fuse, and only answers in time count.
  • It has its own chart of stars. Star every fact in insane mode to unlock 🧮 3-digit mode, with big problems like 347 × 6.

🏆 Leaderboard

  • The leaderboard is a 10 × 10 grid showing who is fastest at each fact right now.
  • Joining is up to you. You can join once 2 facts count: answer a fact right 2 times in a row to make it count.
  • You pick an emoji, a nickname, and two changing device words.
  • Only times from the last 2 weeks count, so keep playing to hold your squares!

📱 Move to another device

  • Players on the leaderboard can move to another device with all their stars, levels and charts.
  • On the new device, tap Use account from another device and enter your nickname and your changing device words. You can find the words on your stats page (the 📊 button).
  • After moving, the leaderboard name works on the new device only.

🔊 Music

Music plays while you answer. Tap 🔊 at the top to turn it off.

Use account from another device

Enter your leaderboard nickname and pick your two changing device words. You'll find them on your stats page on the other device. Your stars, levels and charts will move to this device.
